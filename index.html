Email Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

I am interested in scaling world models to safely bring embodied intelligence to the world. I am an Honorary Associate Professor at UCL teaching “Open-Endedness and General Intelligence” with Tim Rocktäschel and Roberta Raileanu. I am also adjunct faculty at the British Open-Ended Learning & Discovery (BOLD) national UK AI research lab.

I have been working on world models and open-ended learning since 2019, with contributions in my PhD such as Ready Policy One, Augmented World Models and ACCEL. I completed my PhD at the University of Oxford, where my thesis proposed solving embodied AGI by producing an open-ended process training agents inside world models trained from internet data, which I continue to work towards.

In 2022 joined Google DeepMind, where I started the Genie project, the first foundation world model trained from Internet videos, which won a Best Paper award at ICML 2024. We then scaled further with Genie 2, which was featured on CBS 60 Minutes. I was then co-lead of Genie 3, the first world model trained at frontier model scale, and first world model product launched as part of Project Genie. The model was also used by Waymo and won the Grand Prix in Digital Craft at Cannes Lions. Most recently I led Genie 4 pretraining.

For a more formal view of my thoughts, see recent position papers on achieving superintelligence with open-endedness, and leveraging video data for real world intelligence.
